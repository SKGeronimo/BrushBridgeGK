Experimental format support

KPP presets may use complex brush engines, dynamics, textures, and resources. This tool currently targets bitmap tips only, and Krita usually stores a preset’s tip in a separate .gbr file, so add those too (or the .bundle the brushes came from). Textures, dynamics and masking brushes are not converted. If a tip cannot be extracted it is flagged in the list, and you can opt in to using the preset thumbnail instead. The ABR output is Photoshop v6 (tips plus brush presets). Krita’s built-in round and square brushes have no bitmap, so they are approximated with a generated tip.
