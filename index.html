Experimental format support

KPP presets may use complex brush engines, dynamics, textures, and resources. This tool currently targets sampled bitmap tips only. If a tip cannot be extracted, it may use the KPP preview image as a fallback and mark it clearly. The ABR output is legacy version 1 and may not import in every Photoshop release.
